Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email Support@UnderwayApps.com. Include your phone model and what you were doing, and we’ll get back to you.

Common questions

Does SlipMate work without a signal?

Yes. Everything you enter is saved on your phone first and syncs to your account when you’re back online. You need a signal only to sign in the first time, to sync, and to look up places near you.

How do I move to a new phone?

Install SlipMate and sign in with the same account. Your data downloads once you’re online.

How do I delete my account?

See how to delete your account and data.

Where do I send feature ideas?

Use “Suggest a feature” inside the app, or email us above.
