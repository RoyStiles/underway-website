Getting started

Install SlipMate

SlipMate is for iPhone and Android phones.

  1. On your phone, open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  2. Search for SlipMate and install it. You can also open underwayapps.com/get on your phone, which sends you to the right store.
  3. Open SlipMate. You’ll see the sign-in screen.
SlipMate is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. The store buttons on the home page go live when it does.

Create your account

You need a signal for this step, because your account is made on our server.

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Create a New Account.
  2. Type your Email and a Password. Passwords need at least 8 characters. Use the show button in the password box to see what you typed.
  3. Tick Accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Tap the underlined words to read the Terms of Use or the Privacy Policy first. The Create Account button stays greyed out until the box is ticked.
  4. Tap Create Account. You’ll see “Check your email to confirm your account, then sign in.”
  5. Open the email from SlipMate and tap the confirmation link.
  6. Back in SlipMate, tap Have an Account? Sign In, then sign in with the same email and password.
No email? Check your junk or spam folder, and make sure the address was typed correctly.

Sign in and reset your password

  1. Type your Email and Password, then tap Sign In.

Forgot your password?

  1. Type your email in the Email box first.
  2. Tap Forgot Password under the buttons. SlipMate emails you a reset code. Tap OK.
  3. On the next screen, check your Email, type the Code from the email, then your New password and Type it again.
  4. Tap Save New Password, then sign in with the new password.

If you already have a code, tap I Have a Reset Code to go straight to that screen.

Add your boat

The first time you sign in you’ll see Welcome aboard.

  1. Type your boat’s name under Vessel name.
  2. Tap Add My Boat. You’ll land on the Home screen.

One subscription includes up to 2 boats. To add the second one later, see Settings › Boats.

Boat Setup

Boat Setup asks about your boat, engines and systems, then builds a maintenance schedule and a parts list for you to start from. You can skip any step and come back.

  1. On Home, find the Set up your boat card and tap Start setup. (Tap Hide this if you’d rather add everything yourself.)
  2. Each screen shows Step X of Y and a progress bar. Tap ‹ at the top left to go back, or Skip for now at the top right to leave and finish later.

The steps are:

Boat
Type in Search makes and tap your make. Then tap your model. Can’t find it? Tap My make isn’t listed or My model isn’t listed and type it. Add the Model Year (Optional), then Continue. Tap Change to pick a different make or model.
Water & Season
Choose Salt water, Brackish, Fresh water or It varies. Then tick I boat year round, or pick the months your season starts and ends. The months set when Winterization and Spring Commissioning come due; year round skips them.
Engines
Pick the Engine type (Outboard; Inboard/Outboard (Stern Drive/Pod Drive); Inboard (Direct Drive/V-Drive); Jet Drive; or Electric) and How many engines drive the boat? (1 to 4).
Dinghy, Tender and Kicker
Answer whether you have a dinghy or tender, whether it has an engine, and whether you have a kicker motor. A dinghy engine or kicker gets its own services and engine hours.
Pick Your Engines
Tap the engine model for each engine. Horsepower, displacement and cylinders fill in from the catalog and can be changed. Not listed? Tap Other / not listed and type the make and model.
Engine service screens
A list of common services for your engines. See Choosing services below.
Boat Systems
Answer Yes or No for each system your boat has (AC, generator, thrusters, windlass and so on). Each yes adds a screen of services. Seacocks, bilge pumps, steering and batteries are on every boat, so their screens always show.
Pick Your Generator
Shown if you have a generator. Tap the model, or skip it.
System screens, Winterization and Spring Commissioning
One screen of services for each system. Winterization and Spring Commissioning are skipped if you boat year round.
Finished Schedule
A list of every service you kept and its interval. Tap Save Schedule. (Or Finish Without Saving Services to leave without adding them.)

Choosing services. On each service screen:

  • Every service has a Keep tick box, on to start. Untick the ones you don’t want. Select All and Unselect All do the whole screen.
  • The typical interval is shown as a guide. Type your own interval in Every (months or years) and, for engine work, Hours.
  • Tap Show Details for the tasks and notes that go with a service.
  • Under Enter Last Done for Items Below on Save, type a Date and, if you know them, Hours (Optional). When you tap Continue, they’re added to every selected service that doesn’t have its own.
  • A kept service with no interval is marked No Interval Set. It’s saved, but never counted as due until you give it one.
Suggested services, parts and intervals are starting points, not instructions. Always follow your manufacturer’s manual. See Suggested parts and service intervals in the Terms of Use.

To run Boat Setup again (for example after repowering), go to Settings › Boats and tap Run boat setup again for your boat. A rerun updates your schedule; it doesn’t make duplicates.

Getting around

The Home screen

From top to bottom:

  • The blue bar. Tap SlipMate to come back Home from anywhere. Tap the magnifying glass to search. On other screens a back arrow appears at the left.
  • Your boat card. The boat’s photo, name, year, make and model, HIN and registration number. Tap the photo to Take Photo, Choose Photo or Remove Photo. Tap the name to open the vessel details.
  • Boat Readiness inside the boat card.
  • The four areas: Vessel, Maintenance, Destinations and Fuel. Each shows a short summary, such as “2 due · 1 open”. A red number means something needs you.
  • Upcoming Maintenance: the next services due. Tap Schedule to see them all.
  • Next Float Plan: your next outing. Tap New Plan to start one.
  • Recent Activity: your last trip, last fill-up and last engine hours update.
  • At the bottom, when you last synced, or how many changes are waiting for a signal.

Inside an area, the card at the top shows the area’s main lists as big numbers, with its other lists as small buttons underneath. Tap any of them to switch lists.

AreaLists inside it
VesselDetails, Engines, Capacities, Systems, Electronics, Docs
MaintenanceScheduled, Open, Completed, Parts, Manuals (and Switching, on boats that have digital switching records)
DestinationsDestinations, Float Plans, Trip Log, Contacts, Safety Gear
FuelFuel Log, Fuel Efficiency

Boat Readiness

Boat Readiness answers “is the boat ready to go?” It has one verdict and four signals.

  • The verdict: Ready (green), Attention Soon (yellow) or Action Needed (red).
  • Service: scheduled services overdue or due soon.
  • Safety: safety gear expired or expiring soon, plus overdue Fire Suppression and Bilge Pumps services.
  • Parts: parts that are out or below the quantity you want aboard.
  • Docs: documents expired or expiring soon.

Tap a signal to open that list, with the problems at the top.

The bottom bar

Along the bottom of Home and the area screens:

  • Home
  • Service: the Maintenance area.
  • Trips: your trip log and float plans.
  • Safety: your safety gear.
  • Settings: opens the Settings sheet.

Adding, viewing and editing records

Every list works the same way.

  1. Add: tap the blue add button at the top of the list, such as + Add Part, + Add Trip or + Add Fill-Up. A form slides up.
  2. Fill in what you know. Only the first box (the name) is required. Tap Save, or Cancel to close without saving.
  3. View: tap a card to open it and read every field. Links, phone numbers and files can be tapped.
  4. Edit: at the bottom of the record, tap Edit (for example Edit Part). Change what you need and tap Save.
  5. Delete: in the edit form, tap Delete, then Tap Again to Delete within a few seconds.
  • Filters: many lists have a filter button beside the add button (for example Filter: All) to show one component, type or sea condition.
  • Dates: tap a date box to pick a date. Tap Clear to empty it.
  • Positions (Lat/Long): tap the location button beside the box to fill in where you are now.
  • Files and photos: use Add File, Add Photo or Take Photo (where offered), and Remove to take one off.

Search

  1. Tap the magnifying glass in the blue bar.
  2. Type a word: a part number, an engine serial number, a marina, a service name.
  3. Matches show under each section’s name. Tap one to open it.

Search looks through everything on the boat you have open, and works with no signal.

Working with no signal

SlipMate saves every change on your phone first, so you can use it anywhere.

  • With no signal, Home says Offline and how many changes will sync when you’re back online.
  • Changes sync on their own when you have a signal. You can also sync by hand in Settings › Sync.
  • You need a signal to create an account and sign in the first time, to sync, to download a state for Places Near Me, to open a file you have never opened on this phone, and to send a float plan.

Settings

Tap Settings on the bottom bar. A sheet slides up with Account, Boats, Units, Notifications, Sync, Suggest a feature and Theme. Tap one to open it. Tap the X or the dimmed screen to close the sheet.

Account

  • Shows the email you sign in with.
  • Change Email Address: type the New email address and tap Send Confirmation. Tap the link in each email you get (there may be one at each address). Until both are confirmed, keep signing in with your old address.
  • Plan: one yearly subscription that includes up to two boats and every feature, with a 30-day free trial. Starting the trial from inside the app is coming soon.
  • Sign Out: if some changes haven’t synced yet, SlipMate warns you first, because they would be lost. Choose Stay Signed In and sync, or Sign Out Anyway.
  • Export All My Data: see Export all your data.
  • Delete Account: see Delete your account.

Boats

  • Switch boats: tap a boat’s name. The one you’re in has a tick and is blue.
  • Add another boat: type its name in Add another boat and tap Add Boat. One subscription includes up to 2 boats.
  • Run boat setup again for your boat: reruns Boat Setup for the boat you’re in.
  • Review Scheduled Services: see Review Scheduled Services.
  • Delete your boat (red button, for the boat’s owner): four questions in a row make sure you mean it. Are You Sure?, This Is Not Reversible, Have You Exported Your Data? (tap Export First to save a copy) and Last Chance. Tap Delete Boat to finish. The boat and all its records are removed from every phone, and it frees up a boat on your subscription.

Units

Choose how speeds, distances and volumes are shown on this phone.

  • Speed: MPH or Knots.
  • Distance: Miles, Nautical miles or Kilometers.
  • Volume: Gallons or Liters.

Tap a choice and every screen, chart and report follows it. Fuel economy follows too (MPG, NMPG, km/L and so on), and so does fuel burn (GPH or L/h). Changing a unit never changes your records, only how they’re shown, so you can switch back and forth safely. Lengths such as LOA, beam and draft are always in feet. Each phone keeps its own choice.

Notifications

Choose what this phone tells you about. Notifications are made on your phone, so they work with no signal and with SlipMate closed. They never alert anyone else.

  • Float plans only: reminders when a float plan you sent is due back.
  • Float plans and readiness changes: also when Service, Safety, Parts or Docs turns yellow or red.
  • Float plans, readiness and individual items: also each time a service comes due, something expires or a part runs low.

Items with no expiration date entered don’t send a notification. If you see “Notifications are not allowed on this phone yet”, tap Allow Notifications. If they’re turned off in your phone’s own settings, turn them on there.

Sync

Shows when this phone last synced and how many changes are waiting. Tap Sync Now to sync straight away. If a change couldn’t sync, it’s listed here in red with the reason. Email support if it doesn’t clear.

Theme

At the bottom of the Settings sheet, choose Auto (follows your phone), Light or Dark.

Suggest a feature

  1. In the Settings sheet, tap Suggest a feature.
  2. Answer the questions. Only the first one is required.
  3. Tap Send. With no signal, it’s saved and sent when you’re back online.

You can send one suggestion a day. We read every one, but we don’t reply to each.

Vessel

On Home, tap Vessel (or tap your boat’s name on the boat card).

Details

  1. Tap Details in the Vessel card, then Edit Vessel.
  2. Fill in what you know: Vessel name, Make / model, Model year, Year built, Hull type / material, HIN (hull ID number), Registration / documentation #, MMSI (fixed VHF), MMSI (handheld), Home port, Length overall (LOA), Beam, Draft, Deadrise at transom, Displacement / dry weight and Notes.
  3. Tap Save.

The Sales / Survey Report button is also here. See Sales / Survey report.

Capacities

  1. Tap Capacities, then Edit Capacities.
  2. Fill in Air draft (waterline to highest point), the tank sizes (Fuel, Fresh water, Holding tank, Grey water, Water heater), Propane, Battery banks (type, count, Ah), Anchor & rode, Max persons, Max load / max HP rating and Notes.
  3. Tap Save. Tank sizes are shown in gallons or liters, as set in Units.

Engines

Boat Setup adds your engines for you. To add or change one:

  1. Tap Engines, then + Add Engine (or tap an engine, then Edit).
  2. Fill in Position / name (e.g. Port) and Type (Propulsion or Generator).
  3. For a dinghy engine, tick Dinghy engine. For a kicker, tick Kicker. Each gets its own services and hours.
  4. Add Make / model, Serial #, Hour meter now (the starting reading), Horsepower, Max RPM (WOT), Displacement, Cylinders, Engine type, Fuel and Alternator.
  5. For propulsion engines: Transmission model, Transmission serial #, Gear ratio and Propeller (diameter × pitch, blades).
  6. Display order sets the order engines are listed (1 first). Tap Save.

Oil, filters, coolant, impellers and belts are kept as parts, under the Engine/s or Generator component. Tap an engine card to see all its details and its current hours.

Systems and Electronics

Systems is for the equipment aboard (watermaker, windlass, AC unit and so on). Electronics is for your devices (chartplotter, radar, VHF, autopilot).

  1. Tap Systems and + Add System, or Electronics and + Add Device.
  2. Systems: Item, Location, Make / model, Serial #, Installed and Notes (p/n, manual, warranty).
  3. Electronics: Device, Make / model, Serial #, Software / firmware version, MMSI / NMEA 2000 instance / IP, Location, Installed and Notes.
  4. Tap Save.

Docs

Your boat’s paperwork: insurance, registration, captain’s license, boating safety course and inspections.

  1. Tap Docs, then + Add Document.
  2. Type the Name and pick the Type: Insurance, Registration, Captain’s license, Boating safety course, Inspection or Other.
  3. Fill in Issued by and the Policy/Registration/License Number. For a license or course, add the Holder (whose license or certificate). For insurance, add the Coverage (hull value, liability, deductible, cruising area).
  4. Set the Issued and Expires dates. The expiration date drives the Docs signal in Boat Readiness.
  5. Under Copy of the document (photo or PDF), tap Take Photo to photograph the paper, or Add File or Add Photo to attach one.
  6. Tap Save.

Docs that are expired or about to expire are listed first. Tap a doc and then Open copy to show the paper at the dock.

Engine hours

SlipMate keeps a history of hour-meter readings for each engine. An engine’s current hours are its highest reading. Services measured in hours count down from those readings.

Update the hours

  1. Go to Service on the bottom bar. The Vessel Hours card at the top shows each engine’s hours and when they were last updated.
  2. Tap any reading. The Update hours box opens with a box for each engine.
  3. Type each meter as it reads now. You can leave an engine blank if you didn’t check it.
  4. Tap Save.
  • If a reading is lower than the highest one logged, SlipMate asks before saving it.
  • Typed the wrong number? Open the box again and tap Delete last reading.
  • You can also tap Engine hours updated in Recent Activity on Home to open the box.
  • Trips, maintenance entries and fill-ups have hour-meter boxes too. New maintenance entries and fill-ups start with the current hours filled in. Each one you save adds a reading.

How a service counts hours

Each scheduled service has Hours based on:

  • Calendar only: due every so many months, no hours.
  • All propulsion engines (highest): counts the highest of your main engines, for one service that covers them all.
  • One engine: counts just the engine you pick, such as the generator or the port engine.

A service can have both a months and an hours interval. Whichever comes first is when it’s due. To turn one shared service into one per engine, open it, tap Edit, then Split Into a Separate Task for Each Engine.

Maintenance

Tap Service on the bottom bar, or Maintenance on Home.

Scheduled services

Scheduled lists your recurring services in the order they come due: overdue first, then due soon, then the rest. Each card shows Next due and Last completed.

Mark a service done:

  1. Tap Mark done on the service’s card and confirm. It’s logged as completed today at the current hours, added to Completed maintenance, and the next due date starts over.
  2. If the service counts hours and there’s no reading yet, update the hours first.

Done on another day? Open the service, tap Edit, set Last done (and Hours at last done), or tap Log Completed Today / Log Completed at Current Hours, then Save.

Add a service:

  1. Tap + Add Service.
  2. Type the Task and pick the Component. For Engine/s or Generator, pick the Engine when it counts one engine’s hours.
  3. Set Every (months), Every (hours), or both, and Hours based on (see How a service counts hours).
  4. Add Last done, Hours at last done, Performed by and Notes (parts, supplier, procedure).
  5. Tap Save.

Use the filter button to show one component. A service with no interval shows No Interval Set and is never counted as due.

Open maintenance

Open is for jobs you’ve found but not fixed yet: a weeping hose clamp, a sticky bilge switch.

  1. Tap Open, then + Add Open Item.
  2. Fill in Work done (what needs doing), Component, Performed by, Notes and a Photo if it helps. Tap Save.
  3. When it’s fixed, tap Mark done on its card. (Or open it, tap Edit, then Log Completed Today and Save.) It moves to Completed.

Each card shows when it was Opened and how long it has been Open for.

Completed maintenance

Completed is your service history, newest first.

  1. Tap Completed, then + Add Entry.
  2. Fill in Work done, Component, Engine (for engine work), Performed by, Status and Date completed.
  3. Pick a Linked schedule task to reset that service’s last done date.
  4. Add Notes (parts used, invoice #, follow-up) and a Photo. The hour-meter boxes start with the current hours.
  5. Tap Save.

Review Scheduled Services

Add a service you skipped in Boat Setup, or remove one, without running setup again.

  1. Open Settings › Boats and tap Review Scheduled Services.
  2. Pick a Component to see its services and how many are on your schedule.
  3. Tick the ones to add, untick the ones to remove, and set intervals.
  4. Tap Save.

To change your engines or which systems your boat has, run Boat Setup again from Settings › Boats.

Parts on hand

Keep a list of spares aboard and how many you want to carry. Find it under Maintenance › Parts.

  1. Tap + Add Part.
  2. Fill in Part, Part number, Component and Stored where.
  3. Pick Units (Ea, Kit, Set, Pair, Gal, Qt, Box, Misc), then Qty on hand and Qty required (how many you want aboard).
  4. Add Supplier / brand, Specification, Product page (a web link) and Notes. Tap Save.

When Qty on hand drops below Qty required, the part counts as low (or out, at zero) and the Parts signal turns yellow or red. Parts that need restocking are listed first. Used a filter? Edit the part and lower Qty on hand. Tap Product page on a part to reorder.

Manuals

  1. Tap + Add Document.
  2. Fill in Document name and Type (manual, parts list, survey…).
  3. Pick For engine if it’s an engine manual, or fill in Related to (equipment, system…).

  5. Tap Save.

Tap Open file on a manual to read it. Once a file has been opened on your phone, it opens with no signal.

Safety gear

Tap Safety on the bottom bar.

  1. Tap + Add Item.
  2. Fill in Item (flares, fire extinguisher, life jackets, EPIRB…), Quantity and Stored where.
  3. Set Expires / check by.
  4. Tick Track expiration if you want a reminder to show until you enter a date.

Destinations and Places Near Me

On Home, tap Destinations.

Add a destination

  1. Tap + Add New.
  2. Fill in Name, Type (Marina, Anchorage, Mooring field, Fuel dock, Waterfront restaurant, Pump-out, Boatyard / ramp, Bridge / lock, Other) and City / waterway.
  3. Add the Position (Lat/Long), Phone, Website, Email, VHF channel / hail name and Depth.
  4. Add what you want to remember: Hours of Operation, Dockage, Services, Rates and reservations, Approach and hazards, a Marina map (file or link) and My notes (slip #, dock hand, tips).
  5. Tap Save.
  • Tap the star on a card to make it a favorite. Favorites are listed first.
  • Tap a card for its key facts. Tap More to see every field, or Edit to change it.
  • Type in Search destinations… to find one, or use the filter to show one type.

Find Places Near Me

Marinas, fuel docks, anchorages and ramps near you, nearest first. Once your state is downloaded, it works with no signal.

  1. In Destinations, tap Find Places Near Me.
  2. Allow location when asked (or tap Use My Location).
  3. The first time, pick your state under Download a state and tap Download (needs a signal). Tap Remove to delete a state you don’t need.
  4. Narrow the list with the kind filter, the distance (Within …) or Search by name or town….
  5. Tap Map to see a place in your maps app, or Add to My Destinations to save it, where you can add your own notes.
Place listings come from OpenStreetMap, made by volunteers, and can be out of date. Always check with the marina for current depths, hours and prices. SlipMate is not a navigation tool.

Float plans and contacts

A float plan tells someone ashore where you’re going and when you’ll be back. Find it under Destinations › Float Plans (or tap Trips on the bottom bar, then Float Plans), or tap New Plan on Home.

Make a float plan

  1. Tap + Add Float Plan.
  2. Type the Plan title and, if you like, the Route and planned stops.
  3. Pick a saved destination under Leaving from and Going to, or type the Departure port and Destination port.
  4. Set the Planned departure and Expected back date and time. Both are needed to send the plan.
  5. Add the Captain, People aboard (names, phone numbers, medical needs) and Notes (vehicle, trailer, keys location, etc). Tap Save.

Send the plan

  1. Tap the plan, then Send Plan.
  2. Under Who is it going to?, tap a saved contact or New contact. For a new contact, type the Name, Phone (for texts) and Email (for the full plan with the PDF). They’re saved for next time.
  3. Optional: turn on Ask them to call if they have not heard from me and pick how long after your expected-back time (30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or type the hours).
  4. Tap Text Message, Email With PDF or Share PDF. The PDF adds the boat details, engines, people aboard and safety gear aboard, for your contact to give the Coast Guard if needed.
  5. Your phone’s Messages or Mail opens, ready to go. Tap Send there. If SlipMate asks Did You Send It?, tap Sent or Not Sent.

The plan is now marked Sent. If you allow reminders, your phone reminds you when you’re due back, and again an hour later.

A text needs cell service and an email needs internet, so send before you leave signal. You send every message yourself: SlipMate does not monitor trips or alert anyone.

While you’re out and when you’re back

  • Update Return Time: running late (or early)? Set the New expected back time and SlipMate offers to tell your contact.
  • Send All Clear: tells your contact you’re back safe.
  • Complete Trip: fill in Actual departure, Actual arrival, Distance, Fuel added, the engine hours and notes. The plan moves to your trip log. For a sent plan, SlipMate offers to send the all clear.
  • Cancel Plan: removes a plan you didn’t use. For a sent plan you can choose Cancel and Tell to let your contact know.

Contacts

The people you send float plans to, under Destinations › Contacts. Tap + Add Contact to add one with Name, Phone (for texts), Email and Notes, or edit one to fix a number.

Trip log

Tap Trips on the bottom bar. Completed float plans land here too.

  1. Tap + Add Trip.
  2. Type the Trip title and a Short description.
  3. Pick Departed from and Arrival to from your saved destinations (this fills in the port and position), or type the Departure port and Arrival port.
  4. Set Departed and Arrived, and add Distance and Fuel added.
  5. Type each engine’s hour meter when you got back. This adds an engine hours reading.
  6. Add Captain, Crew / guests and Notes (weather, issues, observations). Tap Save.

Each trip card shows the route, date, time underway, hours and distance.

Fuel log

On Home, tap Fuel, then Fuel Log.

  1. Tap + Add Fill-Up.
  2. Check the Date and time. Pick a Saved destination, or type the Location (marina / fuel dock) and City / waterway.
  3. Pick the Fuel type and type the Gallons (or Liters) and Price per gallon (or liter).
  4. Leave Total cost blank and SlipMate works it out, or type the total from the receipt.
  5. The hour-meter boxes start with the current hours. Add Notes (tanks filled, receipt, discounts) and tap Save.

The charts above the list show your fuel cost this year and the price you paid at each fill-up.

Fuel efficiency

Log how your boat runs at different RPMs to learn its best cruising speed. On Home, tap Fuel, then Fuel Efficiency.

  1. Tap Add.
  2. Check the Date and time and pick the Sea conditions.
  3. Type the RPM, % Load, Speed and Fuel Burn (All Engines), the total for all engines, from your gauges or engine display.
  4. SlipMate shows the economy (MPG, or the unit you chose) as you type.
  5. Add Notes (trim, load, current, fuel on board) and tap Save.

In the Fuel card at the top, switch between Chart, Table and Trend. The chart dots are colored by sea conditions. Use the filter to show one sea condition. Tap PDF Report for the Fuel Efficiency report.

Reports

Reports are made on your phone from your records. They work with no signal, and nothing leaves your phone until you share the file.

Fuel Efficiency report

  1. Go to Fuel › Fuel Efficiency and tap PDF Report.
  2. Under Fuel Efficiency Choices, choose:
    • Include what looks worth checking: a short summary of changes over time. It points at what to look at; it doesn’t diagnose a problem.
    • RPM bands to chart: tap the bands you want. Each prints a row of three charts (fuel burn, speed and economy over time). The number on each band is how many readings it has.
    • Past readings go back: the last 3, 6, 12 or 24 months, or all readings.
  3. Pick the paper size, Letter or A4.
  4. Tap Create PDF Report. Your phone’s share sheet opens: save it, print it or send it.

Charts, averages and trends use calm-sea readings only. The past readings list shows every reading.

Sales / Survey report

A tidy report for a buyer or surveyor.

  1. Go to Vessel › Details and tap Sales / Survey Report.
  2. Under What to Include, turn on the parts you want: Engines and drivetrain, Systems, Electronics, Safety gear on board, Documents on file (names only), Spare parts on hand and Fuel efficiency. The vessel details and capacities are always included.
  3. Under Service History, decide whether to include Maintenance schedule and service history. It’s your call: it can help a sale or hurt it.
  4. Under Privacy, choose whether to show Identification numbers (HIN, registration, MMSI; surveyors usually need the HIN) and My notes (off unless you turn it on).
  5. Pick Letter or A4 and tap Create PDF Report.

Documents are listed by name only; no files are attached. Your choices start fresh each time, so nothing private goes out by accident.

Spreadsheet (CSV) files

  1. On the Sales / Survey Report screen, scroll to Spreadsheet (CSV).
  2. Pick the Section: Vessel and capacities, Engines, Systems, Electronics, Safety gear on board, Documents (names only), Service history, Maintenance schedule, Spare parts or Fuel efficiency readings.
  3. Tap Export CSV and share or save the file. It opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

The CSV follows the same privacy choices as the PDF and uses the units you chose in Settings.

Export all your data

Save a complete copy of everything in SlipMate for the boats you own: a spreadsheet (CSV) for every section, plus your manuals, docs and photos in folders, all in one zip file.

  1. Open Settings › Account and tap Export All My Data.
  2. Tap Create Export. It’s made from the copy on your phone, so it works with no signal. A file you’ve never opened on this phone has to download first, which needs a signal.
  3. When the share sheet opens, save the zip somewhere private (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or your computer).
  4. Closed the share sheet too soon? Tap Share Again; it doesn’t rebuild the export.
  5. Tap Remove Export From This Phone when you’re done.
The export holds personal details such as insurance and registration papers and your notes. Keep it somewhere safe. A README inside the zip explains the files and units.

Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently deletes your account, every boat you own and all their records and photos. It can’t be undone, so export your data first.

  1. Open Settings › Account.
  2. Tap the red Delete Account link at the bottom right.
  3. Read the message and tap Delete Everything (or Cancel).

You need a signal for this step. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions too. Can’t get into the app? See Delete your account and data.

Only want to remove one boat? Use Delete in Settings › Boats instead.
