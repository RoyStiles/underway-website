Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

SlipMate is a boat-management app. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: we collect what you enter to run your boat records, we don’t sell it, and we don’t show ads.

What we collect

AccountYour email address, display name and password (the password is stored in scrambled form by our sign-in provider; we can’t read it).
Boat records you enterVessel details, engines and hour readings, maintenance and service history, parts, equipment, documents, safety items, trips, destinations, fuel fills and efficiency readings, and any notes you write.
Photos and filesPhotos and documents you choose to attach (for example parts, marina maps, registration or insurance). The app asks permission first, and only reads what you pick.
LocationYour precise location, only while you’re using the app and only when you use a feature that needs it, such as recording a trip, waypoint or destination position, or finding places near you. We don’t track you in the background.
Messages you send usFeature suggestions, place or catalog submissions, and support emails.

We don’t collect your contacts, advertising ID, browsing history or payment card numbers.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your records, work out due dates, and sync between your devices.
  • To keep your account secure and to respond when you contact us.
  • To improve SlipMate, using the suggestions you send.

We don’t sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Where it is stored

Your records are saved on your phone first, so the app works with no signal. When you’re online they sync to our cloud database and private file storage, hosted by Supabase in the United States. Each account can reach only its own records, and your files are kept in private storage that other users can’t open.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • An email delivery service: sends us your feature suggestions and submissions.
  • OpenStreetMap: marina, fuel dock and anchorage listings come from OpenStreetMap contributors. Your own position is used on your phone to find nearby places.
  • Apple and Google: handle app downloads and, when you start a free trial or subscribe, the payment. We never see your card details.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your records and files are deleted from our systems right away; routine backups expire on their normal schedule. See how to delete your account.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any record in the app at any time.
  • Turn off location, camera or photo access in your phone’s settings; the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Delete your account in the app (Settings › Account › Delete account) or by emailing us.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, at the email below.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or move your data. Contact us and we’ll help.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, accounts are protected by sign-in, and each account’s records are separated at the database level. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

SlipMate is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above, and tell you in the app for significant changes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: Support@UnderwayApps.com
