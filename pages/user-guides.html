Getting started

Install SlipMate

SlipMate is for iPhone and Android phones.

  1. On your phone, open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).
  2. Search for SlipMate and install it. You can also open underwayapps.com/get on your phone, which sends you to the right store.
  3. Open SlipMate. You’ll see the sign-in screen.
SlipMate is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. The store buttons on the home page go live when it does.

Create your account

You need a signal for this step, because your account is made on our server.

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Create a New Account.
  2. Type your Email and a Password. Passwords need at least 8 characters. Use the show button in the password box to see what you typed.
  3. Tick Accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Tap the underlined words to read the Terms of Use or the Privacy Policy first. The Create Account button stays greyed out until the box is ticked.
  4. Tap Create Account. You’ll see “Check your email to confirm your account, then sign in.”
  5. Open the email from SlipMate and tap the confirmation link.
  6. Back in SlipMate, tap Have an Account? Sign In, then sign in with the same email and password.
No email? Check your junk or spam folder, and make sure the address was typed correctly.

Sign in and reset your password

  1. Type your Email and Password, then tap Sign In.

Forgot your password?

  1. Type your email in the Email box first.
  2. Tap Forgot Password under the buttons. SlipMate emails you a reset code. Tap OK.
  3. On the next screen, check your Email, type the Code from the email, then your New password and Type it again.
  4. Tap Save New Password, then sign in with the new password.

If you already have a code, tap I Have a Reset Code to go straight to that screen.

Add your boat

The first time you sign in you’ll see Welcome aboard.

  1. Type your boat’s name under Vessel name.
  2. Tap Add My Boat. You’ll land on the Home screen.

One subscription includes up to 2 boats. To add the second one later, see Settings › Boats.

Boat Setup

Boat Setup takes three short steps: your boat, your engines, and bringing in your data. Everything but your boat’s make and model can be skipped and finished later.

  1. On Home, find the Set up your boat card and tap Start setup. (Tap Hide this if you’d rather add everything yourself. Kept your boat in a spreadsheet? Tap Have a spreadsheet? Import it on the same card: see Import from a spreadsheet.)
  2. Each screen shows Step X of 3 and a progress bar. Tap ‹ at the top left to go back (your answers are kept), the ? for help, or Skip for now at the top right to leave and finish later.
1 Your Boat
Type your make and tap it, then tap your model. Can’t find it? Tap My make isn’t listed or My model isn’t listed and type it. Tap Change to pick a different one. Then, if you like: Model Year (Optional), Hull ID Number (HIN), Registration / Documentation #, Where do you boat? (salt, brackish or fresh water) and your season: tick I boat year round, or pick the months your season starts and ends. A season adds Winterization and Spring Commissioning to your schedule; year round leaves them off. Picking a catalog model also fills in its published specs (water and holding tanks, displacement, max persons) where yours are blank.
2 Your Engines
If SlipMate knows your boat, Are These Your Engines? lists the engines it came with: tap yours, or Different engines if it was repowered. Otherwise pick the Engine type and How many engines drive the boat? (1 to 4). Answer whether you have a dinghy or tender (and its engine) and a kicker; each gets its own services and hours. Then pick each engine’s model (horsepower, displacement and cylinders fill in and can be changed), or tap Other / not listed and type it. Inboard, stern drive and jet engines also have a box for the transmission or drive. A catalog boat may show Standard Equipment to tick; ticked items go to Systems and Electronics.
3 Bring In Your Data
Your boat is saved. Fill your schedule, parts, safety gear and papers the quick way: Import a Spreadsheet, Add a Receipt or Work Order (read with AI), or add things as you go with the + button. Tap Finish Setup.

SlipMate no longer builds a suggested service list during setup. Fill your schedule from a spreadsheet, a work order, or your engine’s manual (Read With AI suggests its service intervals and parts), or add services by hand.

To change your boat or engines later, tap Edit Boat & Engines on the Boat tab (or in Settings › Boats). It keeps what you already entered.

Import from a spreadsheet

Kept your boat in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets? SlipMate can bring it in instead of you typing it again: boat specs and capacities, engines, the maintenance schedule, service history, parts, safety gear, and systems and electronics. For tips on getting a spreadsheet ready, see Keeping your boat in a spreadsheet.

  1. Save or download your spreadsheet as .xlsx or .csv and put it on your phone (in Files, Google Drive, or an email to yourself). Older .xls files need saving as .xlsx first.
  2. Open the import: tap Have a spreadsheet? Import it on the Set up your boat card on Home, or Import a Spreadsheet Into your boat in Settings › Boats. The import fills the boat you’re in, so switch boats there first if you have two.
  3. Tap Choose Spreadsheet and pick your file (see Finding your file if it’s in Google Drive or you can’t see it). Or, for a file in Google Drive or a Google Sheet, paste its link under Or Paste A Google Drive Link and tap Open Link (see Importing from a Google Drive link).
  4. Check The Columns: SlipMate guesses what each tab holds (Boat, Engines, Schedule, History, Parts, Safety or Systems) and which SlipMate field each column goes in, with an example from your sheet beside it. Tap any guess to change it. A column that doesn’t fit can go Add to notes or Don’t import. Tap Preview.
  5. Preview: every row is sorted into Ready, Needs a look (it comes in, with a note on what’s missing) and Skipped (with the reason, such as a row with no name, or something already in SlipMate). Nothing is saved yet. Tap Back to change a match.
  6. Tap Import. The summary shows what came in.
  7. If an engine has no hours yet, tap Enter Engine Hours so hours-based services can count down. Tap any row under Needs A Look to fill in what’s missing.

Finding your file. Always start from SlipMate. Tapping a spreadsheet in the Google Drive, Files or Mail app just opens it in that app; it doesn’t send it to SlipMate. Instead, tap Choose Spreadsheet in SlipMate and find the file in the picker that opens:

  • iPhone: tap Browse at the bottom, then Google Drive under Locations, and open the folder and file. Don’t see Google Drive? Tap ⋯ at the top right, then Edit, switch Google Drive on and tap Done. (The Google Drive app needs to be installed and signed in.)
  • Android: tap ☰ at the top left and choose Drive, then open the folder and file.

When you pick the file you come straight back to SlipMate, on Check The Columns.

Google Drive says “Loading” or “Authentication required”? That message comes from the Google Drive app’s link to your phone’s file picker, not from SlipMate, and it can appear even when you’re signed in to Google Drive. SlipMate never sees your Google account, so the fix is in Google Drive:

  1. Open the Google Drive app, tap your profile picture (top right) and make sure the selected account is the one that has your spreadsheet. Open its folder once, then try the picker again.
  2. Update the Google Drive app, then restart your phone.
  3. Still stuck? Delete the Google Drive app, install it again, sign in and open your files once. This resets its link to the file picker and is the fix that most often works.
  4. Using a work or school Google account? Its administrator may block Google Drive from sharing files with other apps. Use the next tip instead, or ask them.

Still can’t see it, or don’t want to wait? Save a copy on your phone first, then pick that copy. This always works, because Google Drive isn’t involved:

  • iPhone: in the Google Drive app, tap ⋯ next to the file, then Open in, then Save to Files, and choose On My iPhone. In SlipMate’s picker, it’s under Browse › On My iPhone.
  • Android: in the Google Drive app, tap ⋮ next to the file, then Download. In SlipMate’s picker, it’s under Downloads.

A file emailed to yourself works the same way: save the attachment to Files (iPhone) or Downloads (Android), then pick it in SlipMate.

Importing from a Google Drive link. This skips the file picker, so the Google Drive messages above don’t come up. It also works with a Google Sheet as it is, with no need to save it as .xlsx. It needs a signal.

  1. In Google Drive (on your phone or computer), open Share for the spreadsheet. Under General access, choose Anyone with the link, then tap Copy link. Use the link to the file itself, not to the folder it’s in.
  2. In SlipMate’s import, paste it under Or Paste A Google Drive Link and tap Open Link. You go on to Check The Columns as usual.
  3. When the import is finished, go back to Share in Google Drive and set General access to Restricted again. SlipMate reminds you on the summary.
While link sharing is on, anyone who has the link can open your spreadsheet, including your HIN, registration and service history. Turn it off once the import is done. SlipMate never signs in to your Google account, which is why the file has to be shared by link. If you’d rather not share it at all, save a copy on your phone and use Choose Spreadsheet.

“Google Drive did not send the file” means the file isn’t shared as Anyone with the link yet, or the link is to something else. Check the sharing and copy the link again.

No spreadsheet yet, or want the closest match? Tap Get The Template on the first screen. It shares a blank .xlsx with one tab for each list and SlipMate’s column names, to fill in on your computer.

How your sheet is read:

  • Your own column names are fine. “Hrs”, “Job”, “Date done”, “Part #” and the like are matched for you.
  • Units are converted. Liters become gallons, and meters or feet-and-inches become feet. Text such as “$”, “hrs” and the commas in “1,234” is removed.
  • Dates are read US style (3/4/2025 is March 4). A date that isn’t exact, such as “Spring 2024” or just a year, is left blank and flagged rather than guessed.
  • Intervals such as “100 hrs”, “12 months”, “2 years”, “annually” or “100 hrs or 1 yr” are understood. “Seasonal” or “as needed” are flagged, because they never come due.
  • Boat details only fill what’s blank. Your boat’s name and anything you’ve already entered stay as they are.
  • Hours-based services count the highest of your main engines unless the row names one engine (such as Port), matching the engine’s position.
  • Service history is matched to scheduled services by name. A newer entry in the history sets the service’s last-done date. Untick Use the service history for last-done dates on the preview to keep your sheet’s dates.
  • Cost and invoice numbers from the history are kept in the entry’s notes.
  • Importing the same sheet again skips what’s already there, so nothing is doubled.

The import works with no signal: it’s saved on your phone and syncs when there’s a signal.

Change your mind? Tap Undo This Import on the summary, or Undo Last Import on the import screen later. It removes everything the import added, on every phone. Boat details it filled in stay.

Getting around

The Home screen

Home shows what needs doing on your boat today. From top to bottom:

  • Your boat card. The boat’s photo, name, year, make and model, HIN and registration, with Boat Readiness inside it. Tap the photo to Take Photo, Choose Photo or Remove Photo.
  • Get Started (a new boat with no services yet): Import a Spreadsheet, Read a Work Order with AI or Add a Service by Hand. Hide this removes it on this phone.
  • Needs Attention: services that are overdue or due soon. Tap Mark Done when one is finished, or tap it to open it. When nothing is due it says All Caught Up.
  • Next Float Plan: your next outing. Tap New Plan to start one.
  • Engine hours: each engine’s current hours and when they were last updated. Tap them to update the hours.
  • Recent Activity: what was added or done last, such as your last trip, fill-up or hours update. Tap a line to open it.
  • At the bottom, when you last synced, or how many changes are waiting for a signal.

Boat Readiness

Boat Readiness answers “is the boat ready to go?” It has one verdict and four signals.

  • The verdict: Ready (green), Attention Soon (yellow) or Action Needed (red).
  • Service: scheduled services overdue or due soon.
  • Safety: safety gear expired or expiring soon, plus overdue Fire Suppression and Bilge Pumps services.
  • Parts: parts that are out or below the quantity you want aboard.
  • Docs: documents expired or expiring soon.

Tap a signal to open that list, with the problems at the top.

The bottom bar and the top bar

The bar along the bottom:

  • Home
  • Boat: everything about the boat itself, one line per page: Details, Engines, Drives, Capacities, Systems, Electronics, Switching (once your boat has some), Docs, Manuals, Safety Gear and Contacts. Each line shows how many records it has; a red number means expired safety gear or docs. Edit Boat & Engines and the Sales / Survey Report are at the bottom.
  • + (in the middle): Quick Log.
  • Maintenance: buttons at the top switch between Due, Open, History and Parts. See Maintenance.
  • Trips: buttons at the top switch between Plans, Log, Places and Fuel. See Trips.

A red number on a Maintenance or Trips button is how many need you (overdue services, parts to restock).

The blue bar along the top: on Home, your boat’s badge; on other pages, a back arrow. Tap SlipMate to come back Home from anywhere. On the right: the magnifying glass to search, the ? for help on this page, and the impeller to open Settings.

Quick Log (the + button)

Tap + in the middle of the bottom bar for the things you log most. Each opens its form straight away:

  • Log Fuel: a fill-up.
  • Update Hours: the hour meter readings.
  • Log Service: work that’s done, for History.
  • Open Work Item: work to be done, which waits on Maintenance › Open until it’s finished.
  • Add Receipt: read a receipt, invoice or work order with AI.
  • Add Manual: a manual or spec sheet. After you tap Save its page opens, where you tick what it covers and can use Read With AI.
  • New Float Plan and Log Trip.

Tap outside the sheet, or the X, to close it without logging anything.

Help on every page

Every page has a ? in its title bar, and so do the edit forms, Update Hours, Quick Log and Boat Setup. Tap it for step-by-step help with everything on that page. The help is part of the app, so it works with no signal. Tap the X to go back to where you were.

Adding, viewing and editing records

Every list works the same way.

  1. Add: tap the blue add button at the top of the list, such as + Add Part, + Add Trip or + Add Fill-Up. A form slides up.
  2. Fill in what you know. Only the first box (the name) is required. Tap Save, or Cancel to close without saving.
  3. View: tap a card to open it and read every field. Links, phone numbers and files can be tapped.
  4. Edit: at the bottom of the record, tap Edit (for example Edit Part). Change what you need and tap Save.
  5. Delete: in the edit form, tap Delete, then Tap Again to Delete within a few seconds.
  • Filters: many lists have a filter button beside the add button (for example Filter: All) to show one component, type or sea condition. Parts opens on Filter: Required Parts.
  • Dates: tap a date box to pick a date. Tap Clear to empty it.
  • Positions (Lat/Long): tap the location button beside the box to fill in where you are now.
  • Files and photos: use Add File, Add Photo or Take Photo (where offered), and Remove to take one off.

Search

  1. Tap the magnifying glass in the blue bar.
  2. Type a word: a part number, an engine serial number, a marina, a service name.
  3. Matches show under each section’s name. Tap one to open it.

Search looks through everything on the boat you have open, and works with no signal.

Working with no signal

SlipMate saves every change on your phone first, so you can use it anywhere.

  • With no signal, Home says Offline and how many changes will sync when you’re back online.
  • Changes sync on their own when you have a signal. You can also sync by hand in Settings › Sync.
  • You need a signal to create an account and sign in the first time, to sync, to download a state for Places Near Me, to open a file you have never opened on this phone, to send a float plan, and to read a receipt, manual or spec sheet with AI.
  • The help behind each page’s ? works with no signal.

Settings

Tap the impeller at the top right of the blue bar. Settings is one page you scroll: Account, AI, Boats, Theme, Units, Notifications, Data, Sync and Feedback.

Account

  • Shows the email you sign in with.
  • Change Email Address: type the New email address and tap Send Confirmation. Tap the link in each email you get (there may be one at each address). Until both are confirmed, keep signing in with your old address.
  • Plan: one yearly subscription that includes up to two boats and every feature, with a 30-day free trial. Starting the trial from inside the app is coming soon.
  • Sign Out: if some changes haven’t synced yet, SlipMate warns you first, because they would be lost. Choose Stay Signed In and sync, or Sign Out Anyway.
  • Delete Account: see Delete your account.

AI

Turns AI On or Off for your account. It shows the date you agreed, how many receipt scans and manuals you have left, and Review What Is Sent. AI is off until you agree to it. See Reading documents with AI.

Boats

  • Switch boats: tap a boat’s name. The one you’re in has a tick and is blue.
  • Edit Boat & Engines: runs Boat Setup again for the boat you’re in, keeping what you entered.
  • Add another boat: type its name in Add another boat and tap Add Boat. One subscription includes up to 2 boats.
  • Delete your boat (red button, for the boat’s owner): four questions in a row make sure you mean it. Are You Sure?, This Is Not Reversible, Have You Exported Your Data? (tap Export First to save a copy) and Last Chance. Tap Delete Boat to finish. The boat and all its records are removed from every phone, and it frees up a boat on your subscription.

Theme

Choose Auto (follows your phone), Light or Dark.

Units

Choose how speeds, distances and volumes are shown on this phone.

  • Speed: MPH or Knots.
  • Distance: Miles, Nautical miles or Kilometers.
  • Volume: Gallons or Liters.

Tap a choice and every screen, chart and report follows it. Fuel economy follows too (MPG, NMPG, km/L and so on), and so does fuel burn (GPH or L/h). Changing a unit never changes your records, only how they’re shown, so you can switch back and forth safely. Lengths such as LOA, beam and draft are always in feet. Each phone keeps its own choice.

Notifications

Choose what this phone tells you about. Notifications are made on your phone, so they work with no signal and with SlipMate closed. They never alert anyone else.

  • Float plans only: reminders when a float plan you sent is due back.
  • Float plans and readiness changes: also when Service, Safety, Parts or Docs turns yellow or red.
  • Float plans, readiness and individual items: also each time a service comes due, something expires or a part runs low.

Items with no expiration date entered don’t send a notification. If you see “Notifications are not allowed on this phone yet”, tap Allow Notifications. If they’re turned off in your phone’s own settings, turn them on there.

Data

  • Import a spreadsheet into your boat: see Import from a spreadsheet. The import fills the boat you’re in.
  • Sales / Survey Report: see Sales / Survey report.
  • Export All My Data: see Export all your data.

Sync

Shows when this phone last synced and how many changes are waiting. Tap Sync Now to sync straight away. If a change couldn’t sync, it’s listed here in red with the reason. Email support if it doesn’t clear.

Feedback: report a problem or suggest a feature

  1. In Settings, under Feedback, tap Send Feedback.
  2. Choose Report a Problem or Suggest a Feature. The questions change to match.
  3. Answer the questions. Only the first one is required.
  4. Tap Send. With no signal, it’s saved and sent when you’re back online.

Problem reports go to SlipMate Support with your account email, boat, app version, phone and sync status, and we reply by email. You can send up to five a day. A report is sent by sync, so if SlipMate can’t sync, tap Email Support Instead or write to {{supportEmail}}.

Suggestions go to the SlipMate team. You can send one a day. We read every one, but we don’t reply to each.

Boat

Tap Boat on the bottom bar, then the page you want.

Details

  1. Tap Details, then Edit Vessel.
  2. Fill in what you know: Vessel name, Make / model, Model year, Year built, Hull type / material, HIN (hull ID number), Registration / documentation #, MMSI (fixed VHF), MMSI (handheld), Home port, Length overall (LOA), Beam, Draft, Deadrise at transom, Displacement / dry weight and Notes.
  3. Tap Save.

The Sales / Survey Report button is also here.

Fill in from a spec sheet or manual

Instead of typing your boat’s specs, let SlipMate read them from a photo or PDF. You need a signal and AI turned on (Settings › AI).

  1. On Details or Capacities, tap Fill In From Specs or Manual.
  2. Choose Spec Sheet or Photo (a brochure, spec sheet or a photo of one) or Builder’s Owner’s Manual.
  3. Attach the file: Take Photo, or choose a photo (JPEG or PNG, up to 5 MB) or a PDF. Tap Save. The document’s page opens.
  4. Tap Read With AI. When it’s done, tap Review Suggestions.
  5. Each detail, capacity, system and electronic item is shown with your value now beside the one found. Accept, Edit or Reject each one. Nothing changes until you accept it.

Spec sheets of 10 pages or fewer are free. A builder’s manual counts toward your manuals (see Reading documents with AI). The file stays in Manuals.

Capacities

  1. Tap Capacities, then Edit Capacities.
  2. Fill in Air draft (waterline to highest point), the tank sizes (Fuel, Fresh water, Holding tank, Grey water, Water heater), Propane, Battery banks (type, count, Ah), Anchor & rode, Max persons, Max load / max HP rating and Notes.
  3. Tap Save. Tank sizes are shown in gallons or liters, as set in Units.

Engines

Boat Setup adds your engines for you. Each engine card shows its name and current hours; tap it to see its make, model, serial number and specs, then More for its full page or Edit to change it. A small document icon beside the name means it has a manual attached.

  1. To add one, tap + Add Engine.
  2. Fill in Position / name (e.g. Port) and Type (Propulsion or Generator). For a dinghy engine, tick Dinghy engine; for a kicker, tick Kicker. Each gets its own services and hours.
  3. Add Make / model, Serial #, Hour meter now (the starting reading), Horsepower, Max RPM (WOT), Displacement, Cylinders, Engine type, Fuel, Alternator and, for propulsion engines, Gear ratio and Propeller.
  4. Display order sets the order engines are listed (1 first). Tap Save.

An engine’s full page (More) has its history, its transmission, its Manuals (with Add Manual) and its parts. Oil, filters, coolant, impellers and belts are kept as parts, under the Engine/s or Generator component.

Repowered? On the engine’s page, tap Replace Engine (or Replace Generator). Enter the date, the old engine’s final hours and the new engine. The old engine keeps its history; the new one starts on its own hour meter. Choose whether the transmission stays (it keeps its hours) or goes with the old engine.

Drives (transmissions)

One transmission or drive for each engine: Make / model, Engine, Drive type, Serial #, Gear ratio, Gear oil / fluid and when it was fitted. You never type its hours: they come from its engine’s readings, counted from when it was fitted. Add one from the engine’s page, or from Drives on the Boat tab.

A drive’s page has its Manuals and Replace Transmission: enter the date, the engine hours at the swap and the new unit (a used unit can start with hours already on it). The old unit keeps its history, and Restart service intervals from the replacement starts its services fresh.

Services, completed work and parts can be for a transmission (gear oil, bellows); they count hours on its engine.

Systems and Electronics

Systems is for the equipment aboard (watermaker, windlass, AC unit and so on). Electronics is for your devices (chartplotter, radar, VHF, autopilot).

  1. Tap Systems and + Add System, or Electronics and + Add Device.
  2. Systems: Item, Location, Make / model, Serial #, Installed and Notes.
  3. Electronics: Device, Make / model, Serial #, Software / firmware version, MMSI / NMEA 2000 instance / IP, Location, Installed and Notes.
  4. Tap Save.

Tap an item to open its page, where Add Manual attaches its manual. A document icon beside a name means it has one. Boats with digital switching also have a Switching page for circuits and modules.

Docs

Your boat’s paperwork: insurance, registration, captain’s license, boating safety course and inspections.

  1. Tap Docs, then + Add Document.
  2. Type the Name and pick the Type: Insurance, Registration, Captain’s license, Boating safety course, Inspection or Other.
  3. Fill in Issued by and the Policy/Registration/License Number. For a license or course, add the Holder (whose license or certificate). For insurance, add the Coverage (hull value, liability, deductible, cruising area).
  4. Set the Issued and Expires dates. The expiration date drives the Docs signal in Boat Readiness.
  5. Under Copy of the document (photo or PDF), tap Take Photo to photograph the paper, or Add File or Add Photo to attach one.
  6. Tap Save.

Docs that are expired or about to expire are listed first. Tap a doc and then Open copy to show the paper at the dock.

Manuals

Service manuals, owner’s and parts manuals, wiring diagrams, spec sheets, warranties, receipts and surveys, under Boat › Manuals.

  1. Tap + Add Document (or + › Add Manual, or Add Manual on an engine’s, drive’s or system’s page, which links it for you).
  2. Fill in Document name and pick its Type.
  3. Under File, tap Add File (a PDF), Add Photo or Take Photo. Or paste a Link (Google Drive, Dropbox…).
  4. Tap Save.

Tap Open file on a manual to read it. Once a file has been opened on your phone, it opens with no signal.

What a manual is for. On a manual’s page, the For list: tick every engine, drive or system it covers. A manual for twin engines covers both. For the builder’s owner’s or parts manual that covers everything aboard, tick Whole boat. Builder’s owner’s manuals, spec sheets and surveys always cover the whole boat.

Read With AI. On a manual’s page, tap Read With AI to have SlipMate suggest its service intervals and part numbers (and, for a whole-boat manual or spec sheet, boat specs, details and equipment). Tick what it’s for first. A long manual can take a few minutes; you can leave the page. Then tap Review Suggestions:

  • Services land on the items the manual is for (twin engines get one each). A service you already have is updated, shown old versus new, instead of doubled.
  • Parts are matched to your Parts list by part number, then name.
  • Boat specs and details show your value now beside the manual’s.
  • Equipment and electronics already aboard are skipped.

Accept, Edit or Reject each one; each shows the page it came from. What you accept is labeled From your manual.

Safety gear

Tap Boat › Safety Gear.

  1. Tap + Add Item.
  2. Fill in Item (flares, fire extinguisher, life jackets, EPIRB…), Quantity and Stored where.
  3. Set Expires / check by.
  4. Tick Track expiration if you want a reminder to show until you enter a date.
  5. Add Notes and tap Save.

Expired gear and gear expiring soon is listed first and turns the Safety signal red or yellow. When you replace an item, edit it and set the new date.

Engine hours

SlipMate keeps a history of hour-meter readings for each engine. An engine’s current hours are its highest reading. Services measured in hours count down from those readings.

Update the hours

  1. Tap + › Update Hours, or tap the engine hours on Home, the Boat tab or Maintenance › Due. The Update hours box opens with a box for each engine.
  2. Type each meter as it reads now. You can leave an engine as it is if you didn’t check it.
  3. Tap Save.
  • A reading lower than current. Guessed your hours before, and the meter really shows less? Type the real number and tap Save. SlipMate asks first; tap Yes, Use It and the lower reading becomes the current hours. Older readings stay in the history. Only Update Hours does this: a lower number typed on a service, trip or fill-up never lowers the current hours.
  • Typed the wrong number? Open the box again and tap Delete last reading.
  • Trips, maintenance entries and fill-ups have hour-meter boxes too. New maintenance entries and fill-ups start with the current hours filled in. Each one you save adds a reading.

How a service counts hours

Each scheduled service has Hours based on:

  • Calendar only: due every so many months, no hours.
  • All propulsion engines (highest): counts the highest of your main engines, for one service that covers them all.
  • One engine: counts just the engine you pick, such as the generator or the port engine.

A service can have both a months and an hours interval. Whichever comes first is when it’s due. To turn one shared service into one per engine, open it, tap Edit, then Split Into a Separate Task for Each Engine.

Maintenance

Tap Maintenance on the bottom bar. The buttons at the top switch between Due, Open, History and Parts.

Due (scheduled services)

Due lists your recurring services in the order they come due: overdue first, then due soon, then the rest. The engine hours sit at the top; tap them to update.

Mark a service done:

  1. Tap Mark Done on the service’s card and confirm. It’s logged as completed today at the current hours, added to History, and the next due date starts over.
  2. If the service counts hours and there’s no reading yet, update the hours first.

Done on another day? Open the service, tap Edit, set Last done (and Hours at last done), or tap Log Completed Today / Log Completed at Current Hours, then Save.

Add a service:

  1. Tap + Add Service.
  2. Type the Task and pick the Component. For Engine/s or Generator, pick the Engine when it counts one engine’s hours. For gear oil or drive service, pick the Transmission.
  3. Set Every (months), Every (hours), or both, and Hours based on (see How a service counts hours).
  4. Add Last done, Hours at last done, Performed by and Notes (parts, supplier, procedure).
  5. Tap Save.

An empty schedule offers the quick ways to fill it: Import a Spreadsheet, Read a Work Order with AI or Add a Service by Hand. Your engine’s manual can fill it too (Read With AI). Use the filter button to show one component. A service with no interval is never counted as due.

Open (work to be done)

Open is for projects, and things you notice that need fixing, outside your regular scheduled services: a weeping hose clamp, a sticky bilge switch, wiring the VHF to the chartplotter.

  1. Tap Open, then + Add Open Item (or + › Open Work Item from anywhere).
  2. Fill in Work to be done, Component, Performed by, Notes and a Photo if it helps. Tap Save.
  3. When it’s done, tap Mark Done on its card; it’s dated today. (Or open it, tap Edit, then Log Completed Today and Save.) It moves to History.

Each card shows the name, the engine (if any) and your notes. Open items have no hour boxes: the hours belong to the work once it’s done. To record them, edit the entry in History.

History (completed work)

History is your service record, newest first.

  1. Tap History, then + Add Entry (or + › Log Service).
  2. Fill in Work done, Component, Engine (for engine work), Transmission (if the work was on it), Performed by (you, a mechanic or the shop), Status and Date completed.
  3. Pick a Linked schedule task to reset that service’s last done date.
  4. Add Notes (parts used, invoice #, follow-up) and a Photo. The hour-meter boxes start with the current hours.
  5. Tap Save.

Add a receipt or work order with AI

Let SlipMate read the service from a receipt, invoice or work order. You need a signal and AI turned on.

  1. On History, tap Add Receipt/Work Order (or + › Add Receipt).
  2. Take Photo, Choose Photo or Choose File (PDF Or Image). A PDF is read in full, up to 5 MB.
  3. Each suggestion (the work done, its parts and the hour readings) has Accept, Edit and Reject. Accept All accepts every one.
  4. Save. What you accept becomes a History entry, new parts and hour readings, labeled From your receipt. The file is kept in Manuals as a Receipt / invoice.

The shop or vendor goes in Performed by; costs are never saved. A file that isn’t a receipt offers Discard It, Keep As Photo or Choose Type, and uses no scan.

Parts

Keep a list of spares aboard and how many you want to carry. Find it under Maintenance › Parts.

  1. Tap + Add Part.
  2. Fill in Part, Part number, Component and Stored where.
  3. Pick Units (Ea, Kit, Set, Pair, Gal, Qt, Box, Misc), then Qty on hand and Qty required (how many you want aboard).
  4. Add Supplier / brand, Specification, Product page (a web link) and Notes. Tap Save.

The list opens on Filter: Required Parts: only the parts with a quantity required, with any not fully stocked at the top. Choose Filter: All or one component to see the rest.

When Qty on hand drops below Qty required, the part shows Need and how many, and the Parts signal turns yellow (or red, at zero). Used a filter? Edit the part and lower Qty on hand. Tap Product page on a part to reorder.

Reading documents with AI

SlipMate can read receipts, work orders, manuals and spec sheets you choose, and suggest records for you to accept. It’s optional and off until you agree.

  • The first time, SlipMate explains what is sent and asks you to Turn On AI. Turn it off any time in Settings › AI.
  • Only the document you chose is read. Nothing is saved to your boat until you accept it, and each suggestion shows where it came from.
  • Allowances: 10 receipt scans a month, and 10 manuals over 12 months. Spec sheets of 10 pages or fewer are free. A file that turns out not to be a receipt or manual doesn’t count. Settings › AI shows what’s left.
  • It needs a signal. Everything else in SlipMate works without one.

How to use it: receipts and work orders, manuals and spec sheets. See the Privacy Policy for how documents are handled.

Float plans and contacts

A float plan tells someone ashore where you’re going and when you’ll be back. Find it under Trips › Plans, or tap + › New Float Plan, or New Plan on Home. Each plan’s card shows the date first, then where you’re leaving from and going to, then the route and planned stops.

Make a float plan

  1. Tap + Add Float Plan.
  2. Type the Plan title and, if you like, the Route and planned stops.
  3. Pick a saved destination under Leaving from and Going to, or type the Departure port and Destination port.
  4. Set the Planned departure and Expected back date and time. Both are needed to send the plan.
  5. Add the Captain, People aboard (names, phone numbers, medical needs) and Notes (vehicle, trailer, keys location, etc). Tap Save.

Send the plan

  1. Tap Send Plan on the plan’s card.
  2. Under Who is it going to?, tap a saved contact or New contact. For a new contact, type the Name, Phone (for texts) and Email (for the full plan with the PDF). They’re saved for next time.
  3. Optional: turn on Ask them to call if they have not heard from me and pick how long after your expected-back time (30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or type the hours).
  4. Tap Text Message, Email With PDF or Share PDF. The PDF adds the boat details, engines, people aboard and safety gear aboard, for your contact to give the Coast Guard if needed.
  5. Your phone’s Messages or Mail opens, ready to go. Tap Send there. If SlipMate asks Did You Send It?, tap Sent or Not Sent.

The plan is now marked Sent. If you allow reminders, your phone reminds you when you’re due back, and again an hour later.

A text needs cell service and an email needs internet, so send before you leave signal. You send every message yourself: SlipMate does not monitor trips or alert anyone.

While you’re out and when you’re back

  • Update Return Time: running late (or early)? Set the New expected back time and SlipMate offers to tell your contact.
  • Send All Clear: tells your contact you’re back safe.
  • Complete Trip: fill in Actual departure, Actual arrival, Distance, Fuel added, the engine hours and notes. The plan moves to your trip log. For a sent plan, SlipMate offers to send the all clear.
  • Cancel Plan: removes a plan you didn’t use. For a sent plan you can choose Cancel and Tell to let your contact know.

Contacts

The people you send float plans to, under Boat › Contacts. Tap + Add Contact to add one with Name, Phone (for texts), Email and Notes, or edit one to fix a number.

Log (trip log)

Tap Trips › Log, or + › Log Trip. Completed float plans land here too.

  1. Tap + Add Trip.
  2. Type the Trip title and a Short description.
  3. Pick Departed from and Arrival to from your saved destinations (this fills in the port and position), or type the Departure port and Arrival port.
  4. Set Departed and Arrived, and add Distance and Fuel added.
  5. Type each engine’s hour meter when you got back. This adds an engine hours reading.
  6. Add Captain, Crew / guests and Notes (weather, issues, observations). Tap Save.

Each trip card shows the date and time underway first, then where you left from and arrived, then the description, then the hours and distance.

Places and Places Near Me

Tap Trips › Places for your saved marinas, anchorages, fuel docks and more.

Add a destination

  1. Tap + Add New.
  2. Fill in Name, Type (Marina, Anchorage, Mooring field, Fuel dock, Waterfront restaurant, Pump-out, Boatyard / ramp, Bridge / lock, Other) and City / waterway.
  3. Add the Position (Lat/Long), Phone, Website, Email, VHF channel / hail name and Depth.
  4. Add what you want to remember: Hours of Operation, Dockage, Services, Rates and reservations, Approach and hazards, a Marina map (file or link) and My notes (slip #, dock hand, tips).
  5. Tap Save.
  • Tap the star on a card to make it a favorite. Favorites are listed first.
  • Tap a card for its key facts. Tap More to see every field, or Edit to change it.
  • Type in Search destinations… to find one, or use the filter to show one type.

Find Places Near Me

Marinas, fuel docks, anchorages and ramps near you, nearest first. Once your state is downloaded, it works with no signal.

  1. In Trips › Places, tap Find Places Near Me.
  2. Allow location when asked (or tap Use My Location).
  3. The first time, pick your state under Download a state and tap Download (needs a signal). Tap Remove to delete a state you don’t need.
  4. Narrow the list with the kind filter, the distance (Within …) or Search by name or town….
  5. Tap Map to see a place in your maps app, or Add to My Destinations to save it, where you can add your own notes.
Place listings come from OpenStreetMap, made by volunteers, and can be out of date. Always check with the marina for current depths, hours and prices. SlipMate is not a navigation tool.

Reports

Reports are made on your phone from your records. They work with no signal, and nothing leaves your phone until you share the file.

Fuel Efficiency report

  1. Go to Trips › Fuel › Fuel Efficiency and tap PDF Report.
  2. Under Fuel Efficiency Choices, choose:
    • Include what looks worth checking: a short summary of changes over time. It points at what to look at; it doesn’t diagnose a problem.
    • RPM bands to chart: tap the bands you want. Each prints a row of three charts (fuel burn, speed and economy over time). The number on each band is how many readings it has.
    • Past readings go back: the last 3, 6, 12 or 24 months, or all readings.
  3. Pick the paper size, Letter or A4.
  4. Tap Create PDF Report. Your phone’s share sheet opens: save it, print it or send it.

Charts, averages and trends use calm-sea readings only. The past readings list shows every reading.

Sales / Survey report

A tidy report for a buyer or surveyor.

  1. Go to Boat › Details (or the bottom of the Boat tab, or Settings › Data) and tap Sales / Survey Report.
  2. Under What to Include, turn on the parts you want: Engines and drivetrain, Systems, Electronics, Safety gear on board, Documents on file (names only), Spare parts on hand and Fuel efficiency. The vessel details and capacities are always included.
  3. Under Service History, decide whether to include Maintenance schedule and service history. It’s your call: it can help a sale or hurt it.
  4. Under Privacy, choose whether to show Identification numbers (HIN, registration, MMSI; surveyors usually need the HIN) and My notes (off unless you turn it on).
  5. Pick Letter or A4 and tap Create PDF Report.

Documents are listed by name only; no files are attached. Your choices start fresh each time, so nothing private goes out by accident.

Spreadsheet (CSV) files

  1. On the Sales / Survey Report screen, scroll to Spreadsheet (CSV).
  2. Pick the Section: Vessel and capacities, Engines, Systems, Electronics, Safety gear on board, Documents (names only), Service history, Maintenance schedule, Spare parts or Fuel efficiency readings.
  3. Tap Export CSV and share or save the file. It opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

The CSV follows the same privacy choices as the PDF and uses the units you chose in Settings.

Export all your data

Save a complete copy of everything in SlipMate for the boats you own: a spreadsheet (CSV) for every section, plus your manuals, docs and photos in folders, all in one zip file.

  1. Open Settings › Data and tap Export All My Data.
  2. Tap Create Export. It’s made from the copy on your phone, so it works with no signal. A file you’ve never opened on this phone has to download first, which needs a signal.
  3. When the share sheet opens, save the zip somewhere private (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or your computer).
  4. Closed the share sheet too soon? Tap Share Again; it doesn’t rebuild the export.
  5. Tap Remove Export From This Phone when you’re done.
The export holds personal details such as insurance and registration papers and your notes. Keep it somewhere safe. A README inside the zip explains the files and units.

Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently deletes your account, every boat you own and all their records and photos. It can’t be undone, so export your data first.

  1. Open Settings › Account.
  2. Tap the red Delete Account link at the bottom right.
  3. Read the message and tap Delete Everything (or Cancel).

You need a signal for this step. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions too. Can’t get into the app? See Delete your account and data.

Only want to remove one boat? Use Delete in Settings › Boats instead.
