Delete your SlipMate account

Google Play and the App Store ask for this page. It explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SlipMate and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings › Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Download a copy of your data first (recommended). In Settings › Account, tap Export all my data and save the zip: it holds a spreadsheet for every section of your boats, plus your manuals, docs and photos. Once your account is deleted we can’t get it back for you.

Can’t get into the app?

Email {{supportEmail}} from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We’ll delete it and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

Deleted right awayYour account and sign-in, your boats and everything on them (engines, hour readings, maintenance, service history, parts, equipment, trips, destinations, fuel and efficiency records, safety items), and every photo and document you uploaded.
Stays on your phoneA copy of your records stays in the app’s storage on your phone until you uninstall SlipMate or sign in with another account.
May be keptRoutine server backups that expire on their normal schedule, and emails you sent us (support or feature ideas).

Deleting your account can’t be undone. If you have a subscription (including one in its free trial), cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions too, because deleting the account doesn’t cancel billing.
