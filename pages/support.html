Common questions

Does SlipMate work without a signal?

Yes. Everything you enter is saved on your phone first and syncs to your account when you’re back online. You need a signal only to sign in the first time, to sync, to look up places near you, to open a file the first time, and to send a float plan.

How do I move to a new phone?

Install SlipMate and sign in with the same account. Your data downloads once you’re online.

How do I delete my account?

See how to delete your account and data.

How do I report a problem?

In the app, go to Settings › Feedback, tap Send Feedback and choose Report a Problem. It comes to us with your app version and sync status, which helps us fix it faster. Or email us above.

Where do I send feature ideas?

In the app, go to Settings › Feedback, tap Send Feedback and choose Suggest a Feature, or email us above.
